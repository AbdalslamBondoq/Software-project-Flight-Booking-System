## Goal

Model the system context and containers using the C4 model as taught in the course slides, and describe the architecture.

## Key instructions sent to the agent

- "I will send you the diagram and C4 slides so we can fix the diagrams one by one based on the slides."
- "Read the instructions and tell me what they say; then I will tell you which diagram to fix."
- "Why did you choose FastAPI and SQLite? What other options are there? Why not the others?"

## Intended outputs

- C4 Level 1 (context) and Level 2 (container) diagrams in editable Mermaid source.
- A component diagram for the backend.
- `docs/architecture.md` describing the chosen architecture and technologies.

## Acceptance criteria

- The diagrams follow the C4 notation in slide SD07a (element types, technology, description, system boundary, protocols on arrows).
- The diagrams render correctly on GitHub.
- The technology choices are explained.

## Corrections made by the team

- The built-in Mermaid C4 syntax rendered badly; the diagrams were redrawn as flowcharts styled like the slides.
- The first container diagram lacked element types, technologies and descriptions; these were added following SD07a.
- FastAPI and SQLite were first suggested by the agent; the team reviewed the alternatives and decided to keep them.
