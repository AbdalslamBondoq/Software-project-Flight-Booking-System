
# AI Development Log

This log records how the team used AI agents during development, as required by §3.3 of the project instructions. Each row summarises one significant task. The full instructions for each task are in the linked file in [`/prompts/`](../prompts/).

## How we worked with AI

- **Agents used:** Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI).
- **Workflow:** one team member drafted the foundation with an AI agent. The other members then improved it with their own agent. Later, all three members revised the same documents and diagrams together.
- **Review:** the AI produced drafts and suggestions. Team members reviewed every output against the project instructions and course slides, corrected it, and merged it through a pull request.
- **Repeated iteration:** AI output often needed several rounds of correction before it matched our case study and the course notation.
